Privacy Policy

Skinlore · Effective 5 October 2026

Summary

You can use Skinlore without an account. We collect very little, we do not sell data, we show no ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data kept on your device

Your skin profile (skin type, concerns, allergies, pregnancy setting), scan history, favourites, blocked users and settings are stored on your device only. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to read barcodes and ingredient lists. Photos you pick are read on your device to find a barcode or text. Camera images and photos are not uploaded or stored by us.

If you create an account

Signing in with Apple or Google gives us an account identifier and, if you share it, your email address and name. We use them only to run your account. Sign-in is provided by Firebase Authentication (Google).

Reviews

A review contains your nickname, rating, comment, skin type and the date. Reviews are public. They are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). You can edit or delete a review at any time.

Reports

If you report a review or block someone, we store the report: the review concerned, the reason, and your account identifier if you are signed in. Reports are seen only by us.

Product lookups

To show a product we send its barcode or the name you search for to our data providers: INCI API (ingredient analysis) and Open Beauty Facts (names and photos). Product photos are loaded from Open Beauty Facts and retailer servers, which receive your IP address as with any web request. Looked-up product data is cached in Firebase without any personal information.

Usage analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used, for example how many products are scanned, which screens are opened, and whether the upgrade screen is viewed. These events are tied to an anonymous app installation identifier, not to your name or email. They never include your skin profile, the text of your reviews or the products you look at by name. We do not use the advertising identifier.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat manages subscription status for us and receives an anonymous or account identifier and your purchase history for this app. We never see your payment details.

Configuration

The app downloads its settings from Firebase Remote Config, which uses an app installation identifier.

Deleting your data

Profile › Delete account permanently deletes your account and all your reviews, and removes Skinlore's Sign in with Apple authorisation. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. You can also ask us by email.

Children

Skinlore is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not intended for them.

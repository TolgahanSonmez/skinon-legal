Community Guidelines

Skinlore · Effective 5 October 2026

Reviews are public

Anything you write in a review, and your nickname, can be seen by everyone who uses Skinlore. Write about your own experience with the product.

What is not allowed

• Hate speech, harassment, threats or bullying • Sexual, violent or otherwise offensive content • Insults aimed at other people or groups • Spam, advertising, links, phone numbers or email addresses • False or misleading claims, including medical claims • Personal information about yourself or anyone else • Content that infringes someone else's rights

Zero tolerance

We do not tolerate objectionable content or abusive users. Reviews that break these rules are removed, and people who post them can lose the ability to post.

Report and block

Use the ••• menu on any review to report it or to block the person who wrote it. A reported review is hidden for you immediately. A blocked person's reviews are hidden on your device, and we are notified. You can unblock people in Profile.

How we respond

We review every report within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these guidelines and act against the account that posted it. A review reported by several people is hidden for everyone until we have checked it.
